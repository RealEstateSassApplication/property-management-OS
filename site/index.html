From record to resolution

A connected rental lifecycle.

The system is designed to make business events traceable rather than scattering them across spreadsheets and chat threads.

01 / REGISTER

Build your portfolio

Register properties and units, ownership interests and tenancy relationships.

02 / LEASE

Establish the agreement

Maintain tenancy terms and an active lease as the basis for rent obligations.

03 / ACCOUNT

Track the money

Generate obligations, post payments, allocate receipts and review balances and reversals.

04 / RESOLVE

Close the loop

Route maintenance through work orders, quote approvals and documented completion.
